`brain` below is the brain command shown in the SELF-IMPROVING-BRAIN line of your context.

Run `brain review` and show the user what it lists, as a compact table: number, date, what changed, why.

If the user wants something taken back, run `brain undo <number>` for exactly the numbers they name.
Never undo or trust anything on your own, and do not ask the user to approve rules: nothing in this list waits for them. A rule you wrote counts once their own words back it.
