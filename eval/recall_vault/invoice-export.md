# Exporting invoices as CSV for the accounting team

## Export
The finance page offers a monthly export with one row per invoice line. Choose the month, tick include credit notes, download the CSV.

## Columns
Net amount, tax rate, customer number and booking date are always present. Accounting imports the file on the third working day.
