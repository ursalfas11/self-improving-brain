# The on-call rotation and what the pager expects of you

## Rotation
One engineer carries the pager for a week, handover on Monday at ten. A secondary takes over when the primary does not acknowledge within fifteen minutes.

## During an incident
Acknowledge, open the incident channel, post what you know every thirty minutes. Fix first, explain later.
